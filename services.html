Services

How we help families.

Thoughtful legal guidance for the moments that matter most. We focus on solutions, not conflict.

01

Divorce & Separation

We guide you through equitable division, spousal support, and all aspects of the divorce process — with an emphasis on reducing conflict and protecting what matters most.

  • Equitable division of assets and debts
  • Spousal support and maintenance
  • High-asset divorce considerations
  • Business valuation and division
  • Collaborative divorce options
02

Child Custody

When children are involved, their wellbeing is our top priority. We help parents develop custody arrangements that serve everyone's best interests.

  • Legal and physical custody planning
  • Parenting time schedules
  • Modifications to existing orders
  • Relocation disputes
  • Grandparent visitation rights
03

Estate Planning

Protecting your family's future starts with thoughtful planning. We help you create comprehensive plans that safeguard your assets and honor your wishes.

  • Last will and testament drafting
  • Revocable and irrevocable trusts
  • Powers of attorney
  • Healthcare directives
  • Probate administration
04

Mediation

A collaborative, confidential process that helps families reach agreements without the cost and stress of court. We facilitate conversations, not arguments.

  • Divorce mediation
  • Custody and parenting plan mediation
  • Property division mediation
  • Post-decree modifications
