About our firm

Built on a different idea.

That families going through hard times deserve better than aggressive, conflict-driven legal representation.

Our story

From courtroom battles to conversation

Priya Halden spent over a decade at traditional family law firms before realizing that the adversarial approach often caused more harm than good. She saw how unnecessary conflict drained families emotionally and financially.

In 2018, she founded Halden & Voss with a different vision — a boutique firm that combines deep legal expertise with genuine compassion, prioritizing low-conflict solutions that protect both relationships and futures.

Today, the firm serves families across the Denver metro area, helping them navigate divorce, custody, and estate planning with dignity and respect.

Founder

Priya Halden

Esq. — Managing Attorney

Priya is a Colorado-native with a passion for helping families navigate the legal system with dignity. She earned her J.D. from the University of Colorado Law School and has practiced family law for over 15 years.

Her approach combines thorough legal knowledge with emotional intelligence, helping clients understand their options and make informed decisions during difficult times.
